# Model credits

## Cars on the model road (`assets/models/cars/`)

The five cars were modelled in Blender 5.0 for IoT for Young Makers. They contain no third-party models.
Each one is built at real-world size and shrunk 1 : 3 on the road.

| File | Car | Real size (L × W × H) | Author | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sedan.glb` | Silver four-door saloon | 4.80 × 1.83 × 1.45 m, wheelbase 2.83 m | IoT for Young Makers (KundaKode) | Same as this course |
| `suv.glb` | Blue SUV with roof rails | 4.70 × 1.90 × 1.72 m, wheelbase 2.80 m | IoT for Young Makers (KundaKode) | Same as this course |
| `hatchback.glb` | Red compact hatchback | 4.20 × 1.79 × 1.47 m, wheelbase 2.62 m | IoT for Young Makers (KundaKode) | Same as this course |
| `taxi.glb` | Yellow taxi (saloon with roof sign) | 4.86 × 1.83 × 1.45 m (+ sign) | IoT for Young Makers (KundaKode) | Same as this course |
| `van.glb` | White panel van | 5.30 × 2.00 × 2.30 m, wheelbase 3.30 m | IoT for Young Makers (KundaKode) | Same as this course |

### Textures used in the cars

The tyre and rim textures were reduced to 256 × 256 px colour and roughness maps inside each `.glb`.

| Texture | Used for | Author | Licence | Link |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rubber Tiles | Tyre rubber grain | Amal Kumar | CC0 | https://polyhaven.com/a/rubber_tiles |
| Blue Metal Plate | Fine scratches on the alloy rims | Rob Tuytel | CC0 | https://polyhaven.com/a/blue_metal_plate |

The preview renders also used the **Wide Street 01** HDRI by Sergej Majboroda (CC0, https://polyhaven.com/a/wide_street_01).
It is not part of any file in this repository.
