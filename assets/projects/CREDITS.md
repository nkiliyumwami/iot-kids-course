# 3D models and textures on the landing page project cards

These files are used by `assets/projects/projects-3d.js`. Everything else in the scenes (the gate, the Cash Power
meter, the solar light, the water tank and pump, the charcoal stove, the bean garden, the football goal, the bin,
the banana trees and the hills) is modelled in code.

| File | What it is | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `models/sofa.glb` | Velvet sofa | "GlamVelvetSofa", © 2021 Wayfair LLC, Khronos glTF Sample Assets (textures shrunk, Draco compressed) | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| `models/person.glb` | Walking figure | "Xbot" from Mixamo (Adobe), as shipped in the three.js examples | Mixamo licence |
| `textures/wood*.jpg`, `textures/brick.jpg` | Wood floor, brick | three.js example textures | MIT (three.js) |

Khronos glTF Sample Assets: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets
three.js examples: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r128/examples
