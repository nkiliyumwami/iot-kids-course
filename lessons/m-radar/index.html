      Ready to build it to keep? Here is the same circuit on breadboards, so nothing hangs loose (tap 🧩 Breadboard to see it in 3D). You need two half-size breadboards and male–male jumper wires. The pins and the program stay the same.

        Then add these wires (unplug the USB cable first):

          Each numbered column has two strips of 5 holes: a–e and f–j. Holes in one strip are joined underneath, so a wire in c2 touches the pin in a2. The long + and − rails along the edges share power with every part.

          5 The program

          🗺️ Read your Shell map

          Every angle prints one line: the angle, a bar, and the distance. A longer bar means something is closer, like a blip on a radar. This is a sample run (yours will differ):

          
        
          The longest bar is at 60, so the closest thing is about 18 cm away at 60 degrees. The servo ends up pointing there.
