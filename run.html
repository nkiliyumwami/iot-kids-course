Run on my ESP32 BETA

Send your MicroPython code to your real board and watch it work.

1
Plug in your ESP32

Use a USB data cable. The board’s little red light should turn on.

2
Press “Connect my board”

Your browser asks which device to use. Pick the one that says CP2102, CH340 or USB serial, then press Connect.

3
Press “Run on my ESP32”

Start with Blink the blue LED: it needs no wiring. Then change 0.5 to 2 and run it again!

Safety first: only change wiring while the board is unplugged, and ask an adult to check your circuit.

Works in Chrome or Edge on a computer or Chromebook. Your board needs MicroPython installed (once). Nothing you type leaves this device.
